YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

At Dailyform, your privacy matters to us. We are committed to handling your personal information responsibly whenever you visit our website, contact us, or place and order.

01 | Information We Collect

We may collect personal information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, contact details, and order preferences, including your selected product, size, and quantity.

02 | How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process and manage order request, respond to questions and inquiries, confirm product selections and order details, and provide updates regarding your requests.

03 | Order Processing

When you click the Order Now button, your email application opens with your selected product, size, and quantity. You may review and send the order request through email. Our website does not currently process online payments.

04 | Data Protection and Sharing

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information against unauthorized access or disclosure. We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with relevant service providers when necessary to operate the website or handle your requests.

05 | Your Privacy Rights

You may contact us to request access to or correction of your personal information, or to raise concern about how your information is handled, subject to applicable privacy laws.
